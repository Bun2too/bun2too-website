# Website terms and cookie preferences

The public views are `/terms`, `/cookies`, and `/privacy`, linked from the footer and included in public discovery metadata. The operator supplied by the owner is **Bun2too, Inc.**, with legal/privacy contact **info@bun2too.com**. Shared public entity/contact facts live in `src/lib/site.ts`. ## Cookie behavior

`src/lib/cookie-consent.ts` owns the policy version, record validation, 180-day lifetime, and reading-list key. `src/components/CookieConsent.tsx` provides a non-modal first-visit banner, native preferences dialog, footer controls, and shared permission context. `src/styles/cookies.css` contains the focused responsive styles. The provider wraps Auth0 so its state survives Auth0 initialization; it does not gate essential identity or requested support services.

| Category | Default | Current effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Necessary | Active when needed | Remembers the cookie choice; identity, requested support, and security remain available. No unnecessary provider session is created just to display the banner. |
| Preferences | Off | Allows Insights to read/write `bun2too-read-insights` local storage. Without it, marking articles read works only in the current view. |
| Analytics | Off | Loads the environment-configured Google Tag Manager container and GA4 destination for public-site measurement. Reading preferences alone do not load it. |
| Marketing | Off | Separately controls `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data`, and `ad_personalization` when Analytics also permits Google tags. Marketing alone does not initialize the container. No standalone advertising pixel is installed. |

“Necessary only” and “Accept optional” have equal prominence; Customize starts with optional categories off. Closing the dialog, continuing to browse, or accepting site terms does not grant optional permission. A versioned `bun2too_cookie_consent` cookie stores choices and timestamps, without an account ID or email. It is host-only, path `/`, SameSite=Lax, and Secure on HTTPS. It is intentionally JavaScript-readable to gate browser features; it is **not** an authentication or authorization credential. No server consent audit ledger is created.

Malformed, expired, and unsupported-version consent records fail closed. Version `2026-11-08.2` adds separate analytics/marketing choices and asks visitors with the earlier schema again. Cookie choices are rechecked when a tab regains focus/becomes visible and when their expiry is reached. A change in another open tab therefore applies when revisited. Reading-list revocation clears only its key. Analytics/marketing withdrawal also removes accessible matching `_ga`, `_ga_*`, `_gid`, `_gat*`, `_gcl_*`, and `_gac_*` cookies across the current host and parent-domain variants. Unrelated storage, Auth0 transactions, support cookies, provider-domain storage, push subscriptions, and email choices remain untouched. Browser storage failure is handled without breaking public pages; a blocked consent cookie produces an honest persistence message.

`src/components/GoogleTagManager.tsx` and `src/lib/tag-manager.ts` implement **basic consent gating**. No GTM script, tracking iframe, or Google measurement request is initialized before Analytics permission. Before the script is inserted, Google consent defaults are denied and then updated to the saved choice. Advertising signals stay denied without both Analytics and Marketing. Account/API routes, signup embeds, framed pages, and initial query/fragment URLs do not initialize tracking. Previously loaded code cannot be stopped merely by removing a script element: withdrawal, or navigation into an excluded route, updates consent and reloads once with the saved state to unload its listeners/runtime. Tracking denial persists across that reload.

No email, Auth0 user ID, inquiry contents, or support transcripts are placed in the data layer by application code. Initial page-location fields exclude queries/fragments, and referrers are reduced to origin. GTM administrators control published tags and GA enhanced measurement: do not enable form/user-provided data capture or add private fields. Review automatic history/URL collection in Tag Assistant and keep account/callback exclusions in place. Changes to container tags can change data handling without a repository deployment; review and version them accordingly. See [Google consent mode](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/guides/consent), [basic versus advanced behavior](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/concepts/consent-mode), and [GA4 cookie defaults](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11397207).

The public cookie inventory lists current application storage and conditional provider storage. Auth0 lifetimes/names were checked against the installed SPA SDK (`Auth0Client.ts`, `storage.ts`, `transaction-manager.ts`, and `constants.ts`), including its default one-day authentication hints and session-storage transaction. Server support cookies remain signed, HttpOnly, and limited to 24 hours. Update the inventory if SDK or provider settings change. Hosting/security provider behavior must also be verified in the deployed environment; local tests cannot inventory provider-domain cookies created by a live login or edge challenge.

Email subscription consent, inquiry consent, transcript attachment, push permissions, and optional browser storage remain separate. Cookie acceptance must never be repurposed as a newsletter or terms acceptance record. Auth0 remains the source of newsletter preferences; cookie choices are browser/origin-specific, not account-synchronized.

## Adding trackers or storage later

Before introducing additional optional storage, pixels, or embeds, add their purpose and inventory entry and implement their loader behind explicit consent. Update the category schema, parsing, dialog, and withdrawal cleanup together. A marketing checkbox alone does not constrain an arbitrary custom HTML tag: configure consent checks on every GTM tag, including any future non-Google integration. Bump the consent version when a new purpose requires a fresh choice. Never classify convenience or marketing as necessary to bypass the gate. Evaluate applicable regional rules and browser privacy signals for the actual integration.

## Terms drafting and review

The terms are original website-specific drafting, not copied provider contracts. They cover accounts and business authority, signed-service agreement precedence, acceptable use, legitimate public search/AI indexing, site IP, limited submission processing rights, AI accuracy and professional-advice limits, third-party services, suspension, warranty exclusions, a US $100 aggregate free-site liability cap, a business-only indemnity, Texas venue, updates, and contacts. Mandatory consumer rights and non-excludable liabilities are expressly preserved. No mandatory arbitration or class-action waiver is included.

This is a draft for the owner's legal review, not a determination of enforceability or regulatory compliance. Confirm the operator's corporate details, legal contact email/address, Texas/Collin County venue, age restriction, exclusions/cap, indemnity, and jurisdictions actually served before publication. The contact page must provide a functioning, monitored route; the inquiry integration still needs its deployment configuration. A chosen venue is not a claim that the corporation is incorporated or headquartered in Texas.

The site makes terms available but does not currently implement a server-recorded clickwrap acceptance system. The cookie banner is not a substitute. If counsel requires affirmative acceptance, implement a separate versioned acceptance flow and record for the relevant action, including Auth0 Universal Login configuration where appropriate. Do not claim agreement evidence from the presence of a link or an unrelated consent checkbox. Paid engagements need their own agreements and any required data-processing/security terms.

## Verification

Unit tests reject stale/malformed/future/expired consent, prove optional reading storage is not accessed before permission, and check independent Google consent signals, excluded URLs, and targeted cleanup. Fresh-browser checks cover choices, Escape without acceptance, persistence, reopening, and blocked storage. GTM fixtures prove no Google request for necessary/preference-only choices, analytics without advertising, no duplicate loader on an advertising grant, and withdrawal unloading/cleanup while preserving unrelated state. Other tests use a returning necessary-only visitor. Tests do not establish universal legal compliance, verify GA report ingestion, or inventory all live provider-domain cookies.
